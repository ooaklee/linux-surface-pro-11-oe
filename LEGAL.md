# Legal Status

## License and Provenance Decision

- Project-Authored Code License: MIT (Pending final review)
- SP11 UCM Derivation/Redistribution Basis: Upstream ALSA / Local hardware configuration (Pending final provenance audit)
- Existing Per-File SPDX and Licence Notices: Preserve as controlling unless separately and intentionally relicensed (Pending final review)

This records the repository owner's interim direction so ordinary development,
review, pushes, merges, and publication of newly authored artifacts are not
blocked solely because no decision was recorded. It is not a completed legal or
provenance audit, does not replace the licence terms of third-party material,
and does not by itself establish that any particular release is publishable.
Per-file upstream terms and attribution must continue to be preserved while the
final project-licence and SP11 UCM provenance reviews remain pending.
The interim MIT direction does not override an existing per-file SPDX identifier
or licence notice, including the repository's current BSD-3-Clause notices.

## Working Policy for New Licence Questions

When development encounters another unresolved licence or provenance question:

1. reference an existing recorded basis when it genuinely applies;
2. otherwise append a specific entry to this file with the best currently
   supported basis;
3. label the unresolved decision `(Pending final review)`; and
4. include that entry in the next coherent, reviewed push, then continue normal
   development, merges, and newly-authored-artifact publication while
   preserving all third-party notices, per-file terms, provenance references,
   and attribution.

Recording a pending item prevents an undocumented policy gap. It does not turn
an unknown third-party licence into permission, supersede an incompatible
licence, or certify that a final legal/provenance audit has occurred.
